ANNUAL FUNDRAISER

Austin C. Machitar Golf Tournament

  • Monday, October 5
  • 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Riverwalk Golf Club

About the Event

A day on the course at Riverwalk Golf Club in Austin's memory, with all proceeds funding the Austin C. Machitar Scholarship for a graduating senior in the Academy of Criminology and Justice at Otay Ranch High School.

Registration includes 18 holes with a cart and range access, a lunch buffet and drinks, on-course contests and raffle prizes, and the closing reception.

Set along the winding San Diego River in the heart of Mission Valley, Riverwalk is a 27-hole layout by Ted Robinson Sr. and Jr. — mature trees, water features, and meandering fairways, minutes from Downtown. Plan to arrive early to check in and warm up on the range.

FUNDRAISER

Redondo Family Tennis Clinic

  • Saturday, October 17
  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Canyon Crest Academy
  • Skill level 3.0 – 4.5+

About the Event

A fun 2-hour guided tennis clinic led by the Redondo Family coaches. Featuring rotational drills, skill development, and engaging on-court activities designed to improve your game. All proceeds from this clinic aim to support the Austin C. Machitar Scholarship for a graduating senior in the Academy of Criminology and Justice at Otay Ranch High School.

Light refreshments, snacks, food, and prizes are included.

Austin was an accomplished tennis player himself — a talent inherited from his mother Leslie, of the famed Redondo tennis family. This clinic brings that family back to the court in his honor. Plan to arrive a few minutes early to check in and warm up before the first rotation.
